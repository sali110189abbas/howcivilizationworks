How Civilization Works

The hidden systems behind everyday life.

How Civilization Works is an independent YouTube channel that explains the systems the modern world runs on: power grids, infrastructure, supply chains, the internet, water and money. We explain how they work, how they fail, and the engineering that keeps them running.

Watch: youtube.com/@howcivilizationworks
How we make videos. Every video is researched from official reports and primary sources, and we list those sources in each video's description. Diagrams and maps are drawn by us. Narration uses an AI voice reading a script we write.
Tools. We use a private internal tool, "HCW Uploader", to upload and manage videos on our own YouTube channel through the YouTube API. It is not offered to the public. See our privacy policy.
